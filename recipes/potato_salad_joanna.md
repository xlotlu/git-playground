# Potato Salad (Joanna Recipe)

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes
- 3 tbsp cider vinegar
- 3/4 cup chicken stock
- 2/3 cup finely chopped onion
- 1/2 cup finely chopped dill pickle
- 3 hardboiled eggs, sliced thin
- 4 tbsp finely chopped fresh chives
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise (Best Foods)
- 1/2 cup sour cream

## Instructions

1. Cut potatoes in half, cover with water and add 1 tbsp salt. Boil for 10–15 minutes until just cooked through. Drain.
2. When cool enough to handle, cut into large bite-size pieces.
3. Mix the vinegar with chicken stock and drizzle over the warm potatoes. Let stand for 10 minutes to absorb the liquid.
4. Mix mayonnaise and sour cream together first. Add all ingredients to the potatoes and combine gently. Adjust seasoning and refrigerate for 1–2 hours.

## Recipe Information

- **Prep time:** 30 minutes
- **Servings:** 6–8
